Basis: DP-0.11 (prohibitions), DP-0.14 (merge owners by role; "Founder only for the decision queue and external gates"), Delivery §6/§6.1 and the checklist. The package does not define a "tech lead" role. The PROPOSAL here is that the tech lead holds `integration/waggle-next`, assigns roles from the canonical DP-0.14 list (Harness, Chat, Durable, Memory, Server, Boundary, Capability, Security, External-executor, Evolution, Model/Runtime, UX, Attention, Channels, Benchmark, Release, OSS/License owner) to specific people, and arbitrates between them. The package also does not say who appoints the tech lead, through which channel escalation happens, or who assigns the DP-0.14 roles (UNKNOWN). ### 5.1 The developer decides alone (within an approved ticket)

- Implementation within the ticket contract (FRD requirement, AT, files from Delivery §2), the design of the RED test and the minimal GREEN.
- Rewriting a test that pins currently wrong behavior, **in the same PR** and with a rationale in the PR description (DP-0.15). Silently deleting a test is not allowed.
- Internal names, local refactoring within the PR diff and the choice of fixtures, provided they do not change the public contract, schema, event payload or behavior outside the ticket.
- Stopping work when any checklist item reads "no". This is an obligation, not a choice.

### 5.2 Ask the tech lead (and the role owner)

- Merge into `integration/waggle-next` and the order of merges on hotspot files (DP-0.14); splitting or combining PRs.
- Items marked "open — owner's engineering decision (role)", e.g. the timezone field in ADR-07 O4 (Durable owner, MIG-02), wire-vs-remove for `AgentLearning.formatLearningPrompt` (Evolution owner), `npm audit` advisory or blocking (OSS/License owner, OSS-PR4), BvB Q1/Q6.
- Any change to a schema, event payload or public API contract that is not in the ticket and does not touch a D-nn, the G scope or an ADR decision.
- Expiry of a time-box (e.g. W1-PR1): the tech lead packages the partial results for RAT-02. The time-box is not silently extended.
- A deviation of the measured duration from the estimate by more than the upper bound of the ticket's range. The tech lead reports the impact on the G date to the founder.
- A contradiction between documents of the package. The tech lead records it, and if it touches the G scope or the checklist, escalates it to the founder. If it touches a D-nn, the tech lead reports it to the founder per §5.3 (report, without reopening).
