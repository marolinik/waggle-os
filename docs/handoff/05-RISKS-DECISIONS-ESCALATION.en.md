### 5.1 The developer decides alone (within an approved ticket)

- Implementation within the ticket contract (FRD requirement, AT, files from Delivery §2), the design of the RED test and the minimal GREEN.
- Rewriting a test that pins currently wrong behavior, **in the same PR** and with a rationale in the PR description (DP-0.15). Silently deleting a test is not allowed.
- Internal names, local refactoring within the PR diff and the choice of fixtures, provided they do not change the public contract, schema, event payload or behavior outside the ticket.
- Stopping work when any checklist item reads "no". This is an obligation, not a choice.

### 5.2 Ask the tech lead (and the role owner)

- Merge into `integration/waggle-next` and the order of merges on hotspot files (DP-0.14); splitting or combining PRs. - Any change to a schema, event payload or public API contract that is not in the ticket and does not touch a D-nn, the G scope or an ADR decision.
- Expiry of a time-box (e.g. W1-PR1): the tech lead packages the partial results for RAT-02. The time-box is not silently extended.
- A deviation of the measured duration from the estimate by more than the upper bound of the ticket's range. The tech lead reports the impact on the G date to the founder.
- A contradiction between documents of the package. The tech lead records it, and if it touches the G scope or the checklist, escalates it to the founder. If it touches a D-nn, the tech lead reports it to the founder per §5.3 (report, without reopening).
