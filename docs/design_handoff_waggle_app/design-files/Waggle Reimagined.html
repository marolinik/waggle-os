A design concept for Waggle OS

The hive,
made calm.

Waggle is a genuinely powerful agent platform — persistent memory, model-agnostic orchestration, a swarm protocol. But today it speaks like an engineer and looks like a dashboard. This is a concept for making it feel premium, intuitive, and progressively revealed — calm for the knowledge worker, with every ounce of power one keystroke away.

The read

What's working —
and what's in the way.

The bones are strong. The friction is in density, language, and surface noise. Five things stood out reading the live product.

01

The spine is overloaded

The left nav carries five zones and ~18 destinations before you've done anything. For a non-technical operator, that's a wall of choices — and the most important act, talking to your agent, is just one item among many.

observed18 nav items · 5 zones · always visible
02

It speaks fluent engineer

"Spawn agent", "Waggle-Dance", "MCPs", "GEPA", "consolidated memories". Power users get it; the operator you're designing for stalls. The product hides its own benefits behind its vocabulary.

observedjargon-first labels, no plain-language layer
03

Everything is small and the same size

The cockpit runs on 10–13px type with thin borders around every block. Nothing leads. It reads as a control panel, not a calm daily briefing — and it never feels premium at that density.

observedtext-[10px]…[13px] · border on every card
04

Five colors shouting at once

Blue, purple, green, orange, red all fire simultaneously across the briefing, layered over glass, a wallpaper, and a honeycomb texture. The eye has no rest and no hierarchy to follow.

observed5 semantic hues + glass + wallpaper + comb
05

The magic stays hidden

Memory that compounds, work that happens overnight, agents that coordinate — these are the reasons to choose Waggle. They're present but buried in counters and lists. The differentiators never get a hero moment.

observedbenefits expressed as small tallies
The response

Five principles
for the redesign.

Each one answers a finding above. They're the rules every screen in this concept follows.

01 ↦ findings 01·02

Calm by default,
powerful on demand

Show only what the moment needs. Swarm, MCPs, optimizer and the rest live behind one command bar — discoverable, never in the way. Progressive disclosure as the core mechanic.

02 ↦ finding 01

One clear spine

Collapse 18 destinations into a handful of plain-language places, anchored on the conversation. Everything else is reachable from ⌘K — the OS, not the sidebar, holds the breadth.

03 ↦ finding 05

Show the magic

Memory, overnight work and coordination get real estate and language a human understands. The differentiators become legible scenes, not tallies in a corner.

04 ↦ findings 03·04

Warm & crafted

A warm graphite hive, one honey accent used with intent, an editorial serif for moments that matter, and air between things. Premium is restraint plus a real type hierarchy.

05 ↦ finding 02

Speak human, reveal the expert layer

Lead with plain words — "Start a task", "Working while you're away", "Team of agents". The technical name ("waggle-dance", "MCP server") rides along as a quiet subtitle for the power user. One product, two reading levels.

Identity

A warmer hive.

Same DNA — honey, hexagons, the waggle dance — rebuilt for warmth and restraint. The cold blue-graphite becomes a warm graphite you'd want to live in; honey stops being decoration and becomes a precision accent; and one clean grotesk does the talking — simple, not bookish.

The engagement layer · why it sticks

Built to pull you back.

A product that compounds deserves a habit loop. Trigger → action → variable reward → investment, made concrete with a daily streak, a compounding-memory counter, "while you were away" wins, and earned nudges. Momentum, not manipulation — every loop is anchored to real value, and all of it can be turned off.
