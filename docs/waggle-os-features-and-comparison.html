🐝 Waggle OS — the full picture

Everything it does, how each part works, and how it stacks up.

Waggle is an AI assistant that never forgets. Below: every major feature in plain language (what it does and how it works), followed by an honest comparison with four other AI agents.

💡 The one idea to hold onto: most AI tools forget you the moment a chat ends. Waggle keeps a permanent memory for every project — and can even sit on top of the other AI tools you already use, remembering across all of them.

1 · It remembers everything

The part nobody else does well. This is the core — and it's free forever.

🧠Persistent memory ("Hive-Mind")
Remembers every fact, decision, and preference — across days, weeks, and sessions.
How · Everything you tell it is saved as a "frame" in a local database. When you ask something, it finds the relevant memories by meaning and by keyword, links people & things into a knowledge graph, and keeps a live picture of who you are and what you're working on now.
🗂️Workspaces
A separate, sealed space for each project. What's discussed in one never leaks into another.
How · Each workspace has its own private memory. Start from one of 15 ready-made templates (Sales, Legal, Engineering, Finance…) that pre-load the right setup, and switch between workspaces freely.
📥Harvest — import your past
Bring in your old ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, PDFs, and web pages so it knows your history on day one.
How · Adapters read each export, strip duplicates, and an extraction step ("Cognify") pulls the meaningful facts straight into your memory.

2 · It does the actual work

Not just chat — it researches, writes, codes, and plans, and can run a whole team of agents.

👤Specialists / personas (22)
Switch the assistant's expertise on demand — researcher, writer, legal, finance, coder, analyst, and more.
How · 8 universal modes (including a read-only Planner, an adversarial Verifier, and a Coordinator that runs other agents) + 14 domain specialists matched to your workspace. Each has its own instructions, tools, and guardrails — e.g. the Verifier can only critique, never edit.
🛠️Real tools, not just talk
Web research, reading/writing files, running code, generating Word docs, building step-by-step plans.
How · Each specialist gets a curated toolkit (the Coder gets Git; the Writer gets document export) and a safety filter controls exactly what each one is allowed to touch.
🐝Teams of agents (WaggleDance)
For big jobs, it runs several agents in parallel and coordinates them.
How · A Coordinator breaks the job into pieces, spawns specialist workers, gathers their results, and a Verifier checks the output before it reaches you.
🧬It gets better over time (Evolution)
The system learns to do recurring tasks better — not just the same thing every time.
How · It runs variations of a task, a judge scores them, an optimizer keeps what works, and improvements roll out behind safety gates.

3 · It connects, extends, and protects

Plug in your apps, sit on top of your other AI tools, and keep it all on your own machine.

🛒Marketplace (skills + connectors)
Add new abilities, and connect your apps — Gmail, Slack, Notion, GitHub, databases, and more.
How · Install skills and connectors from a catalog; connectors securely sign in to your services. This is the main paid upgrade.
🔌Works WITH your other AI tools
Detects, launches, and connects other AI agents — Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor, Hermes, OpenClaw — so their work feeds your memory too.
How · It installs reversible "hooks" into those tools; every session they run gets captured into your Hive-Mind. Waggle becomes the shared memory above all of them.
🔒Private & local
Runs as a desktop app; your memory lives on your own computer, not someone else's cloud.
How · A desktop binary with a local database; secrets in an encrypted vault; strict security — injection defense, sandboxing, no secret leakage.
🪜Plans & Enterprise (KVARK)
Free forever for memory; paid for skills/connectors/teams; enterprise runs sovereign on your own infrastructure.
How · Trial → Free → Pro ($19/mo) → Teams ($49/seat) → Enterprise = KVARK, the on-prem version where data never leaves your perimeter.
