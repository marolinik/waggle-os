## §1 — Context

**ADR-08-K1 (DECISION — D-01, D-02, D-03, D-05 · PROPOSAL — BRIEF DIRECTION — DIR-20).** DECISION: Waggle is free/open-source for the individual (D-01); there is no separate Waggle Team/Enterprise product — team and enterprise capabilities come **by connecting to KVARK** (D-02), which remains exclusively on-prem (no cloud fallback; D-03); BYOK remains in individual Waggle (D-05). Future billing for an individual convenience service has not been abolished, but it is not this plan's job (brief §3 footnote). PROPOSAL — BRIEF DIRECTION (planner direction, not user approval): "Team through KVARK, no tier pretense" (DIR-20): a flow is needed for connection and identity validation, allowed organizational capabilities, token storage and revocation; `ENTERPRISE=true` in the local config is not sufficient. This ADR **implements the decisions in the existing code**, and elaborates the brief direction as the working basis of the draft.

## §2 — Decision (implementation proposal)

**ADR-08-O1 (PROPOSAL) — tier is not a permission boundary for the individual.** `Tier` remains a **legacy billing/compat field** in `config.json` (readers do not break), but individual controls, new or existing, must not depend on it: approvals, per-workspace cost, local audit export, sessions, connectors, workspaces, skills, Loops/routines, evolution, memory. `PermissionEnvelope` (ADR-04 O5) has no tier layer. `TIER_CAPABILITIES` is not extended with new flags.

**ADR-08-O3 (PROPOSAL) — KVARK connection as the capability boundary (DIR-20, A27, AT-26).** Contract `KvarkConnection { baseUrl, identity, tokenRef: 'vault:kvark:connection', validatedAt, health, allowedOrgCapabilities: string[], sharingPolicy: 'none' (default), revokedAt? }` (FRD-11.4). **ADR-08-O4 (PROPOSAL) — no cloud fallback and no spillover (D-03; AT-26; ADR-05 O3; ADR-06 O8).** Unavailable on-prem KVARK → `KvarkUnavailableError` → "workspace memory only" (existing `handleKvarkError`), **never** a switch to a personal BYOK/cloud model or to a cloud judge; "Connect to KVARK" does not copy the personal mind, private emails or personal runs; disconnect/revocation terminates further access, including the cached organizational context per policy.
