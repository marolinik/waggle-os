The proposals are PROPOSAL and are not approved. No row is closed by an aggregate count or by a founder confirmation; the founder decides only a row with the status "awaiting an actual founder decision".

A "ticket blocker" blocks only the named ticket or gate and is recorded in that ticket's gates in `backlog.csv`. The deadline is the ticket, PR or checkpoint before which the row is resolved.
